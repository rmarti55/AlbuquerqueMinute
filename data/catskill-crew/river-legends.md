RIVER LEGENDS

Today is a big day… Catskill Crew launches its very first video game.

Introducing: RIVER LEGENDS.

The goal of the game is simple, but it is far from easy: 10 casts to claim the top spot on the leaderboard.

From sturgeon and striped bass to walleye and trout, there are dozens of species. But what lurks below the surface is mysterious and shrouded in legend.

Ancient creatures that many believe to be merely myth and lore. Others claim they only appear when the conditions are just right — and when your cast is perfect.

The changing weather patterns bring new opportunity, and if you get caught in a thunderstorm, be ready — you never know what may appear.

The herons along the bank, eagles soaring overhead, and celestial objects streaking across the sky — all omens of what may come.

Now, it’s your turn to step forth, test your skill, and etch your name on the River Legends leaderboard.

Tight lines.

P.S. This game has taken a lot of time, and I couldn’t have done it without the help of the brave testers who helped squash all the bugs.

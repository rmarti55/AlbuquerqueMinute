Greatest Hot Toddy Recipe 🍹

Quick history lesson. The Toddy was a category of drinks that dates back to the 1700s (someone double fact-check me on that), and it was quite simple, consisting of the spirit, a little bit of sugar, and water. Over time, it has evolved and it became fashionable to drink it hot, thus the Hot Toddy was born. But we can do better than that…

What You’ll Need

- Scotch (I use a blended malt whiskey called Monkey Shoulder, but this is dealer’s choice.)

- Honey Syrup which is a 3:1 honey to water ratio

- Fresh lemon juice

- Chamomile tea

- Cinnamon in stick form

- Whole cloves

- A lemon

Recipe

- Make the chamomile tea, set it off to the side and let it steep

- Get your mug, temperate with some hot water, then dump it out

- Pour 1.5 oz of spirit into the hot mug

- Add .5 oz of your honey syrup

- Add .25 oz of your fresh lemon juice

- Drop in your cinnamon stick

- Top it off with your chamomile tea which should be about 4 oz

- Cut a lemon peel, express the oils, and rub it around the rim

- Then punch a few cloves through the peel and lay it across the top of the drink

- Now chug it down and make another!

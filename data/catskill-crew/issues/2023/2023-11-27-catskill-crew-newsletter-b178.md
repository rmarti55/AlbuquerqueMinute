Winter is Upon Us  ❄️

November 27th / Events can change, cause things happen.

Hope y’all enjoyed your turkeys and tofurkeys. We got a bunch more folks added to the list over the weekend, so welcome!

Before we get into, just one quick little itsy bitsy thing…Please keep sharing this newsletter with friends and/or enemies, thanks 🙏

The Mountains Are Open 🏔️

Gif by nowness on Giphy

The lifts are running over at Hunter, Windham, and Belleayre, who just introduced their shiny new Overlook Quad Lift and a fresh blue named Goat Path. Best get shredding and pray for some snow!

- Tubbys, Kingston - June McDoom with Crosslegged Will Straton

- Colony, Woodstock - Pressing Strings with The Rollin’ Rust, show kicks off at 7pm. Followed by Gimme Gimme Disco, The Abba-theemed DJ dance party at 10pm

- Belvedere Inn, Stamford - Samonik will be playing a live set with Tempo Twist

- Bethel Woods Center, Bethel - Holiday Hootenanny Fridays in the Hub from 7pm-8pm

- Pearl Moon, Woodstock - Rock n Roll with Finding Alice at 9:30pm

Saturday 2nd

- Slopes, Hunter - Saturday Night Slopes with special guest DJ starts at 8pm

- Graveside Variety, Woodstock - Escape your holiday blues with BLEAK, Songs for the Not-Quite End of the World. With John Coons and Matt Aument at 9pm more info

- Phoenicia Playhouse, Phoenicia - Free Winter Open House with movies and hot cider.  Christmas Comes But Once a Year as well as Scrooge more info

- Tubbys, Kingston - Another Michael, Jodi, and Eli Waltz

- Pearl Moon, Woodstock - Flash Band 9:30pm

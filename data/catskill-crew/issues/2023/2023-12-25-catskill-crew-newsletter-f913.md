Creative Spotlight 🕯

As we continue to shine a light on the truly amazing and inspiring folks that call the Catskills home, that next creative force is none other than Jamie Kennard.

Introducing Jamie Kennard, a genuine luminary of the Catskills and a master of many trades. Over the past two decades, Jamie has dedicated himself to shaping brands and propelling organizations to greatness through Krate, his dynamic creative studio. Notable clients include Aether Apparel, French Embassy, Cornell University, Clove & Creek, and many more.

In 2015, Jamie co-founded Brunette, a waterfront wine bar that garnered acclaim from Bon Appétit, Zagat, Vogue, Food & Wine, Eater, and Saveur—making it a must-visit spot.

But that's not all; Jamie's notching up an impressive streak of over five hundred 5ks in a row, conquering them on skis, bikes, hikes, runs— any way he can. Beyond his athletic feats, Jamie is a documentarian, a bird-watching extraordinaire, a skier that reaches summit before the sun crests over the peaks, and a truly skilled photographer (his work has graced the pages of Flylords as well as And North).

Be sure to check out his Instagram for a front-row seat to his next adventures and explore his captivating prints that would look great on any wall.

Take a Hike 🌲

No seriously, go take a hike with the DEC’s First Day Hike Series. There are two options to choose from on January 1st.

- 4 mile loop through an old stone quarry and up to the summit of Bramley Mountain with amazing views of the western Catskills.
Meet in Delhi at the Bramley Mountain trailhead at 11am with Alexa Tumbarello - more info

- A shorter option is a 0.5 mile loop to the Upper Esopus Fire Tower with David Hans. Meet in Mt. Tremper at the Visitor Center at 11am more info

Both hikes are hosted by The Catskill Center and they will be giving away free swag and one free NYS Parks Empire Pass at each hike.

HISTORY OF TAXIDERMY

The first Catskill Crew: Social Studies event has arrived, and we will be graced by the incredible, brilliant, and inspiring BRANT MACDUFF for a riveting lecture on the History of Taxidermy.

About Brant MacDuff: Brant is a conservation & taxidermy historian who studies and writes about wildlife conservation strategy & economics. He recently launched a book — The Shotgun Conservationist — and just got back from Mongolia, where he was studying how "safari style" economics benefit wildlife and people in the western Altai mountain range.

Now, he’s taking us on a journey through the quirky and intriguing history of taxidermy, highlighting key moments in the world of this unique art and the evolution of early museums. You'll hear about some of the most important and eccentric figures behind the wildlife conservation movement in America, with a wild story to go with every slide.

Come on out, meet some humans, have a beverage, grab a bite, and learn some stuff. We’ll see you at The Arnold House in Livingston Manor on Friday, May 8th at 6pm. Want to stay the night? Get 30% off your stay on May 8th and 9th with the code CATSKILLCREW.

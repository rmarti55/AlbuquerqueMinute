THE EAGLES

A few months ago we sponsored 6 farm animals (Tuesday, Penny, River, Peppermint, Leroy, and Tiny) — now we’ll sponsor the fiercest animal of them all: a kids soccer team.

The Northern Catskill Youth Association, aka the Rip Van Winkle Soccer Club, is a nonprofit that offers year-round soccer programs to get kids ages 4 to 18 on the field.

For 25 years, the club has kept some of the lowest registration prices around — so every kid who wants to play, can play.

They rely on donations and volunteers to make that happen. So Catskill Crew is stepping up to sponsor a team for the 2026 season and we need $400 to support the mission. 100% of the proceeds raised go directly to the club so If you can, please please please make a donation here.

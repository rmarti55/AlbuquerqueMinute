JOIN THE TEAM

After nearly two and a half years of building Catskill Crew solo, the time has come for me to explore bringing on some support - and what better place to start than right here.

I’m looking for one amazing human to join me on this daring mission: to build a deeper connection to our land, our history, and our community, all while using fun as our North Star.

I have big plans for the future of Catskill Crew, and it’s a journey that I cannot accomplish on my own. So, if you’re interested in joining the Catskill Crew team, or perhaps you know someone who would be a great fit, you can learn about the role via the link below.

I'm not rushing this — whether it happens today or six months from now, I'm waiting for the right person.

JOIN THE TEAMFARM FRIENDS

How about this… We raise some money to adopt/sponsor local farm animals that are in need. In the process, we support amazing local organizations that are giving them their second chance.

Based on preliminary research we can help → pigs, cows, goats, chickens, rabbits, horses, and more.

It’s not very expensive, for example, we can sponsor a cow for a whole year with only a few hundred bucks.

If this gets approved, I’ll share next steps so we can begin the process to support our new farm friends — and obviously organize some hangs to actually meet them.

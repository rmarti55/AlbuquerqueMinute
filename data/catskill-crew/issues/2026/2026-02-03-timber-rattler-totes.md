Welcome back. Let’s take a moment to extend our appreciation to Clark’s Dairy Farm in Delhi who announced their creamery is closing up shop. Thanks to Kyle and the team — which includes the cows — for everything you’ve done, and all of those the top-tier dairy products (that cold brew…). I, like many others, hope this is only temporary. Wishing you all the best. Now, let us begin.

TIMBER RATTLER TOTES

After months of testing - the totes have arrived. They’ve been battle tested and are ready for whatever you can throw at ‘em or in ‘em.

Heavy duty 24A canvas. No bells. No whistles. No zippers to break. No fancy lining to rip. Just rivets, canvas, an outer pocket, and an inner pocket.

They are limited edition, so when they’re gone, they’re gone forever.

If you want one, you should probably get one and I’ll be shipping them out immediately.

TIMBER RATTLER TOTESLAST CALL

Reservations close in just a few hours for the next Catskill Crew Dinner Club in Woodstock.

Take the quiz, get matched, and come out to meet some new friends and see some neighbors. 

JOIN THE DINNER CLUBFARM FRIENDS

The votes were close and the results are in: Penny the cow took the lead, followed by Leroy the pig, and then Tuesday the goat.

But I say… we sponsor ALL three of them and do our best to support the good people over at the Woodstock Farm Sanctuary.

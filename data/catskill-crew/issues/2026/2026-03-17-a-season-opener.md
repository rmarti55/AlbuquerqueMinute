A SEASON OPENER

There was a time, not all that long ago, when fly fishing in the Catskills had proper opening and closing days — anglers would circle the dates on their calendars, dust off their gear, and gather streamside to mark the changing of seasons.

These days the season runs year-round, but we still honor the tradition and bidding farewell to the cold weather — with good people, great food, and a grand old time.

Join us April 3rd from 5–9pm at The Arnold House to kick off the fly fishing season. We'll have cold beverages, mighty eats, DJ Whipoorwill spinning vinyl, and a raffle in partnership with Theodore Gordon Flyfishers to support conservation in the Catskills.

And if you're wondering — “Do I need to fly fish?” Nope. Not at all. So come on out and lets celebrate another season on the water.

SEARCH & RESCUE

These are the folks you hope you never need — but if you do, you'll be glad they exist.

Catskill Mountain Search and Rescue is a team of trained ground search and rescue volunteers who assist law enforcement when someone goes missing or gets injured in the Catskills and beyond.

This team is 100% volunteer, and they could use our support. Every dollar donated goes directly to training, gear, communication tools, and medical supplies — the things that keep them ready to respond when the call comes in. CMSAR is a 501(c)(3), so your donation is tax-deductible.

If you've ever hiked these mountains, fished these rivers, or wandered these trails, consider giving back to the people who make sure we all come home safe. Donate today.

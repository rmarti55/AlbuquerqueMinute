PARTNER WITH CATSKILL CREWPROTECTING THE WILD

A few days ago, I saw a fawn get hit by a car. I sat with her for a few hours hoping she'd get up on her own but it was clear she wasn't going to.

As the sun set and a storm rolled in from the west, I called Friends of the Feathered & Furry Wildlife Center (FFFWC) up in Hunter. Within 30 minutes, I met Dave on the side of the road and passed the fawn into their care.

She's a tough girl, still fighting the good fight, and I'm grateful she's in their hands.

FFFWC is a 501(c)(3) wildlife rehab run by Barbara "Missy" Runyan — a multi-licensed Wildlife Rehabilitator who's been doing this for over 20 years. Bears, owls, foxes, fawns, songbirds, turtles, fisher cats — animals come to her from all over the state.

If you can, please support these great humans and their important mission. 100% of donations go directly to animal care. Donate here.

And save the number for the next time you find an injured feathered or furry neighbor: 518-965-1864.

The very first Mushroom Madness event is coming to town and it's going to be a grand time.

The day will be full-on mushroom stuff from morning to night at Hilltown Commons in Rensselaerville — a special place dating back over 100 years. Sprawling across 90 acres, there are trails, beautiful residences, a theatre, a lake, a pub… and a bunch of goats that just kinda follow you around if they get out.

We'll be foraging with John the mycologist from Catskill Fungi, crafting mushroom paper with Roberta from BlueSpruceWandering, enjoying a beer tasting with Return Brewing, jamming to vinyl with DJ Harlan, and enjoying good food from Lil' Piece.

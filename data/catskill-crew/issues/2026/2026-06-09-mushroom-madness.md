MUSHROOM MADNESS

The time has come. The very first Mushroom Madness event is coming to town and it's going to be a grand time.

I teamed up with the amazing humans at Hilltown Commons in Rensselaerville (which, if you haven't been, you should fix that).

The property is a special place — dating back over 100 years and best described as a campus for the curious. Sprawling across 90 acres, there are trails, beautiful residences, a theatre, a lake, a pub… and a bunch of goats that just kinda follow you around if they get out.

The day will be full-on mushroom stuff from morning to night. We'll be foraging with John the mycologist from Catskill Fungi, crafting mushroom paper with Roberta from BlueSpruceWandering, enjoying a beer tasting with Return Brewing, jamming to vinyl with DJ Harlan, and enjoying good food from Lil' Piece.

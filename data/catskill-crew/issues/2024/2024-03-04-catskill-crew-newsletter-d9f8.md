🎣  Tie a Fly

I like to describe winter fly fishing as enjoyable suffering. You usually cant feel your fingers or toes and the fish are slower moving and less apt to take the fly. Over the past few weeks, I’ve been having the most success with the Frenchies. It’s a highly effective winter fly pattern that’s fairly easy to whip up on the vice - I mean, if I can do it, you can do it, trust me. With some warmer temps on the horizon, it’s a good time to get out on the rivers. So tie ‘em or buy ‘em and go have some fun.

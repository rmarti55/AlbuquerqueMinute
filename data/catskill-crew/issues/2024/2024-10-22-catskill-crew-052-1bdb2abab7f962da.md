☄ Orionids

The Orionid meteor shower, originating from debris left by Halley's Comet, will be visible during the pre-dawn hours on the 22nd, with a peak of around 15 meteors per hour. To see the the show, look towards the Orion constellation just before dawn. These suckers are fast, they are bright, and they often leave glowing trails in their wake. With clear skies and minimal light pollution, it's a great opportunity to enjoy a light show.

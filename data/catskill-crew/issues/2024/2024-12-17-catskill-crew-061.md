The West Kortright Center

Since 1975, the West Kortright Center has been bringing world-class performers and diverse voices to a small corner of the Catskills. The historic stage has hosted an incredible range of talent, from avant-garde icons like Allen Ginsburg and John Cage to roots legends like Odetta and global music stars like Dakha Brakha. Beyond performances, the Center serves as a hub for community gatherings, educational workshops, art exhibitions, and a renowned youth theatre program that has launched countless careers from New York to Hollywood. As the Center approaches its 50th anniversary in 2025 (and the building's 175th!), it remains true to its founding mission of bringing the creative arts to the local community - including the neighboring cows! Those interested can follow the WKC newsletter for upcoming events and plans to renovate the space during this milestone year.

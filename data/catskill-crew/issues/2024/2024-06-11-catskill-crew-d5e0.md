🍹 Recipe Time: The cocktail shaking, drink slinging legends over at the Catskill Cocktail Club, shared the recipe for one of their favorite warm weather sippers. Go make one for yourself and report back on what you think.

❝MISS KITTY 😺
Served over pebble ice in a rocks glass
✺ 1 oz Wray & Nephew White Overproof Rum
✺ 1 oz Pineapple-sage shrub
✺ 1/2 oz lime juice
✺ 3/4 oz float Pollinator Spirits Crimson Amaro
✺ Garnished with a half-salted rim, pickled pineapple and a mint sprig

👨‍🔬 Chugability Report

Subject: Wayside Cider, Half Wild

Rating: 17 / 10 stars ⭐ 🌟 ⭐

Notes: I believe I’ve tried all of Wayside Cider’s options at this pt and my favorite remains their Half Wild. Made of locally harvested wild and dessert apples, it’s the least dry cider they make and has some sharp acidity. If you haven’t tried it yet, get yourself to the taproom in Andes and give it a shot.

🛶 Delaware River Days

Over the weekend I camped, fished, and got drenched by torrential rain while canoeing 12 miles down the Delaware River. The fishing was good, the Coors Banquets were crisp, and the dark skies turned to clear misty nights as we sat around the fire. This is the perfect way to say farewell to summer and welcome the cooler temperatures of fall. Hope y’all make some time to sleep under the stars.

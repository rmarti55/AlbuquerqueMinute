🛣 ROAD TRIP UPDATE

It’s been HOT. Made it down to New Mexico where I fished the Costilla Creek, then further south to Carlsbad where the temps topped out at 105 degrees and I pulled a big “NOPE”. Turned right back north and am now in Telluride along the San Miguel River with Catskill’esque temps of 50 degrees. In the final leg of the journey and then headed back East to the sweet-sweet Catskills!

🛣 ROAD-TRIPPING

Currently writing this newsletter from a cabin along the Arkansas River in Colorado. I’m in phase 1 of my annual roadtrip from the Catskills out west, in pursuit of more trout. Last night, as the sun was setting, I missed a few bites on some dries, but managed to solve the fishy puzzle by tying on a large grasshopper pattern and bouncing it off the banks along the undercut of the swift moving waters. Rapid fire hit and a miss as the trout started going crazy.  Finally, I hooked into a few beautiful brown trout and brought them to net, which brought me some solace as moments later I snapped my rod climbing my way back out of the thick brush and cacti-laden riverbank.

So send any recommendations for 5 weight rods as I’m reluctantly forced back into being in the market. Luckily, I still have a 4 and 6 weight in the trout wagon.

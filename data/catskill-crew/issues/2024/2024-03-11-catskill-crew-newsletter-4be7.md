🎟 Update: 1st Happy Hour

Well my friends, I’m excited to announce the details for the Inaugural Catskill Crew Happy Hour… DRUM ROLL PLZ 🥁

The first one will be held on Tuesday, March 26th at The Urban Cowboy Lodge in Big Indian.

Good Times + Good People

Here’s the deal: We got a DJ, drink specials, snacks, and perhaps some more fun stuff, oh, and the restaurant will be open as well. We’ll presell 40 tickets for $20 each, which get you a free welcome shot and eligibility for a $99 room rate-special so you can really have some fun (first come first serve). Don’t have a ticket? No problem. You can still come by and pay at the door - but sorry, no welcome shot for you and that post Happy Hour room will be $149 (subject to availability).

I hope to see you there so I’m not awkwardly sipping martinis alone. If you want to guarantee your ticket, click the link and say YES and if you want a ticket and a room say HELL YES 🤠

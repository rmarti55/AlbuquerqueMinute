☄ Panther Mountain Meteor

You may have heard the theory that 375 million years ago, during the Devonian period, a meteor crashed down into a shallow sea, forming a crater that eventually became Panther Mountain. There has been a lot of research to validate the 7-mile crater theory - and I ended up going down a rabbit hole and wanted to share. Take a look below. If it peaks your interest, keep digging because there is a lot more info out there. Warning: it’s a bit dense.

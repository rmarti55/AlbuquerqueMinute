James Beard Awards 🧑‍🍳

The 2024 Restaurant and Chef Awards semifinalists have just dropped for the James Beard Awards — and Upstate is being represented by some culinary legends!

But if you don’t know what the James Beard Awards are yet, here is a quick TL;DR.

The James Beard Foundation is a nonprofit organization with a mission to celebrate, support, and elevate the people behind America’s food culture and champion a standard of good food anchored in talent, equity, and sustainability. Established in 1990 and first awarded in 1991, the James Beard Foundation’s Restaurant and Chef Awards are one of five separate recognition programs of the James Beard Awards. The awards recognize exceptional talent and achievement in the culinary arts, hospitality, media, and broader food system, as well as a demonstrated commitment to racial and gender equity, community, sustainability, and a culture where all can thrive. The 2024 Restaurant and Chef semifinalists are recognized across 22 categories.

So who from upstate made the cut? Lets take a look shall we…

Keep it Moving 🏃

I’ve always been a proponent of exercise in any form, and recently had the  opportunity to connect with the folks behind Hill 99 and thought it may be of interest to some of you! So, I asked Erica, the mastermind behind the club, to share some words that I can pass on here.

"Have promises slipped through the cracks this year? The Hudson Valley's newest running/walking club, Hill 99, is here to transform aspirations into achievements. Inspired by Hill 99 in Woodstock, they are a not-for-profit community encouraging movement, nature, and connection.

Join them as they challenge each other to achieve something extraordinary 99 times—walk or run at least 99 miles in 2024! They invite individuals to create their own Hill Challenge or Walk the Hill 99 in Woodstock, meeting fellow Hillers. Memberships include a progress-tracking wall calendar, an honorary Hill 99 sticker, and a commemorative pinning ceremony at year-end

They aren't just about numbers; they are also a new community offering monthly meetups both on and off the Hill, socials, and a support group to keep participants inspired. Just walking a mile a day can boost health, and setting and achieving goals is invaluable. Join them in conquering your challenge, one step at a time!

Join Hill 99 Club today at www.Hill99club.com, and let the high-fives begin!"

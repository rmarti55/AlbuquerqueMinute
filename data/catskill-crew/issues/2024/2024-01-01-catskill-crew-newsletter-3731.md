Happy Monday

January 1st

Events can change, because things happen and I’m not perfect…

Gif by nowness on Giphy

Happy 2024, folks! Hopefully, you made it to midnight and rang in the new year with a toast. Meanwhile, I was asleep by 10 pm.

Looking forward, I’m excited to see where Catskill Crew goes this year, and with your help, feedback, and support, I’m confident we can keep growing this into something much more than just a newsletter. Rather a platform that celebrates the Catskills, the people that call this place home, and all the amazing things that happen here.

I really appreciate the positive messages I keep receiving from you all as we surpass the 700 subscriber mark and continue to grow.

I wish you — yeah, you — a very happy, healthy, and adventurous new year. Now let’s get into it!

-Michael Kauffman 🎣

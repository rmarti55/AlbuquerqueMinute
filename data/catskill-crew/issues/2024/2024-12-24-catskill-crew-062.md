Crew Card #002 Has Landed!

Well my friends, you asked and we delivered - the second edition of our Crew Card is here! We've teamed up with 10 incredible spots across the mountains to help you discover some local gems while saving some cash. Each spot is a 10% discount, valid through 2025.

The lineup (and holy smokes, it's good):

- Deer Mountain Inn, Tannersville: Cozy mountain vibes with top-notch dining

- Hamden General, Hamden: Your favorite country store (and sandwiches)

- Harana Market, Accord: Filipino flavors meet Catskills charm

- Morning Sunshine, Ellenville: Coffee, groceries, and brunch dreams

Mark your calendars for December 30th - we're getting a New Moon to close out 2024. This is when the Moon plays hide and seek with the Sun, becoming completely invisible to us earthbound observers as it passes between Earth and our local star. For all you photographers and stargazers out there, this moonless night offers prime conditions for capturing the Milky Way or doing some long-exposure shots of our mountain landscapes under the stars.

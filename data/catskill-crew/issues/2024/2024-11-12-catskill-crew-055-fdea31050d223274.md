Supermoon Season

November 15th brings us the Beaver Moon, the final supermoon of 2024, lighting up our night sky bigger and brighter than your average full moon. The name comes from Native American traditions, marking when beavers built their winter lodges and indigenous peoples set traps before the waters froze.

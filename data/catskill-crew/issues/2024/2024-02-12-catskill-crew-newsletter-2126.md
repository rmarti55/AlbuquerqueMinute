💳  Catskill Crew Card Lineup

So last week, we announced the Catskill Crew Card and the feedback has been great… so thats sweet!

The next step was partnering with ten local businesses who would offer one-time discounts for the first card. Thats now done. Drum roll please 🥁

Catskill Crew Card Lineup:

🇪🇸 Mirador

🍺 West Kill Brewery

🤠 Urban Cowboy

🪓 Catskill Outpost

🍔 The Pines

🛍 Catskills Curated

🍸 Nat’s Mountain House

🤩 Shandaken Inn

🍷 Hudson Wine Merchants

❤‍🔥 Home Goods of Margaretville

There will only be a limited number of cards for sale (100-150). Price is to be determined, but I want this to be a “no brainer” type of deal for you all. So, if you want to be first in line to know more and get your hands on a card, email me and just say YES, and if you already reached out, I got you on the list and will be in touch soon, promise!

Also, we teamed up with the local legend, Steven Weinberg, to give away one of his latest prints to a lucky Catskill Crew Card holder.

I’m excited to keep suggesting new ideas, and if you like them, we’ll run with them, and if you hate them, well that’ll be that.

Always feel free to reach out, say hi, suggest your favorite spots you want covered, etc etc!

-Michael 🤠

“Kaatscast is a biweekly series featuring Catskills culture, history, sustaibility, local interview, literature, and arts. The shows are hosted by Bretty Barry and produced by Silver Hollow Audio, in the heart of the Catskills. It was voted “Best Regional Podcast” two years in a row.”

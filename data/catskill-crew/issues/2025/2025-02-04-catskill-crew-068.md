Night Sky: Moon Meets Pleiades

Mark your calendar for February 5th - the first quarter Moon will have a close encounter with the Pleiades star cluster in Taurus starting around 11:00pm. While visible to the naked eye, grab your binocs if you have them; if you have a telescope, dust it off - they'll transform the Pleiades from what looks like a tiny Big Dipper into a dazzling collection of stars. Fun fact: these stars formed together about 100 million years ago, and their blue glow comes from starlight illuminating an ancient cosmic dust cloud.

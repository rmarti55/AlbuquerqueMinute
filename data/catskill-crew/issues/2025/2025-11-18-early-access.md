DINNER CLUB

The votes are in and the motion has passed: The Catskill Crew Dinner Club is officially a go with a 96% approval rating.

Work is underway and I’m hoping to announce the inaugural dinner next week (fingers crossed). Given the sheer size of the Catskills, I’m thinking we start the party in the “big city” aka Kingston. If we deem it a success, we can launch more across the region.

EARLY ACCESS: QUEEN OF THE CATSKILLS

I partnered up with local legend and creative wizard, Jamie Kennard, to hook up Crew Members with early access tickets to the local premiere of his new film Queen of the Catskills.

The premiere will be at Tinker Street Cinema in Woodstock on Saturday, December 13th. Proceeds from the ticket sales help support Winter4Kids, whose mission centers on access and equity in outdoor sports and learning. GET YOUR TICKETS HERE.

GIVEAWAY

The winner of the Catskill Crew x Keep Good Company Records giveaway is KATHY C. Check your email, because you just won 2 tickets to the Lost Leaders + Ginger Winn concert at Park Theatre this Friday, along with a bunch of goodies.

There are only a few tickets left for this Friday. So, if you want to join the fun, grab your tickets!

THE QUARTERLY

After our 97% approval vote, I spent the last two and a half months tinkering, writing, and designing. Today… I’m excited to announce the launch of The Catskill Crew Quarterly.

These snail mail letters will arrive each season to your door, stuffed to the brim with Catskill Goodness. Inside, you’ll find: A mini-almanac, drink and food recipes, limited edition stickers (one is indeed camo glitter), a coloring page, a crossword puzzle, and an invitation to a private event with two drink tickets.

Four envelopes per year. Four adventures. Each season, something new. For each individual letter that goes out, I will plant a tree. So, be among the first to receive the Quarterly. I’m really excited about this, and hope you all enjoy.

P.S. The Catskill Crew Newsletter will always be 1,000% free. I’m not interested in pay gates or reserving content for paid subscribers. But snail mail is simply too fun.

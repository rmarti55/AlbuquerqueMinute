LOCAL SECRETS: REVEALED

For decades, the Catskills’ most pristine swimming holes and trout-packed fly fishing honey holes — have been protected by an unspoken code. A sacred pact passed down through generations of locals who swore never to share them outside the circle.

No pins. No names. No hints. Just nods, whispers, and the occasional hand-drawn map on a napkin. Until now…

Catskill Crew is proud to present: A Local’s Guide to Top Secret Swimming Holes and Fly Fishing Spots — featuring 100+ fiercely guarded locations, each revealed with exact coordinates, and yes, even parking instructions.

These aren’t your average “hidden gems” already geotagged into oblivion. These are the real ones, the hush-hush, sacred-ground spots locals only share after a multi-year vetting process and at least one blood oath.

The vault is open. The dam has broken. And we’re handing you the map.

So grab your rod, your tube, your loudest friends, and a six-pack of glass beer bottles.

Available today only — April 1st

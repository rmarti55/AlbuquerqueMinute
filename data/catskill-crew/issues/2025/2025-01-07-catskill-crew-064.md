Quest: The Best Burger

My fellow Crew members, I need your help…

I’m embarking on a noble and daring quest to uncover the absolute best burger in the Catskills. This isn’t just a mission; it’s a calling - a destiny I’ve been preparing for my entire life.

Suggest a BurgerChugability Report: Winter Negroni

It’s cold out there so here's something to warm your bones. A bit of a winter spin on the classic Negroni we all know and love.

What You'll Need:  1 oz each: London Dry Gin, Campari, Sweet Vermouth, fresh rosemary sprig, orange peel, 2 dashes orange bitters, and one big ice cube

The Move: Clap that rosemary between your palms to wake up the oils, stir everything (except that garnish) with ice for 30 seconds, pour over your big cube, hit it with an orange peel twist, and drop in the rosemary.

Pro tip: Let the rosemary hang out in your gin for 24 hours if you're feeling fancy. Trust the process.

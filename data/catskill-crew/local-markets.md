FIND THE BLOODROOT

Bloodroot (Sanguinaria canadensis) is a classic spring ephemeral wildflower native to the Catskills. It blooms early, sets seed, and goes dormant by mid-to-late summer as the canopy closes. You can spot it in mature hardwood, floodplains, slopes, and thickets.

You may have expected to see a red flower… but no… the name comes from the bright red or reddish-orange sap that oozes from its underground rhizome when cut.

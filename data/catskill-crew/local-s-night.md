BOSCOBEL HOUSE & GARDENS

Just south of Cold Spring, set on 68 acres overlooking the Hudson River, sits Boscobel House & Gardens — a Federal-period landmark from the years just after the Revolution, saved from demolition in the 1950s and rebuilt piece-by-piece on the bluff where it stands today.

Embark on a guided tour, where expert guides bring to life the craftsmanship, stories, and people that shaped Boscobel more than two centuries ago. Then wander the formal gardens, the Native Meadow, or the Woodland Trail that leads down to Constitution Marsh — one of NY State's largest bird sanctuaries.

Boscobel also hosts the Boscobel Chamber Music Festival, now in its fifth season: the 2026 program explores the shared roots of European and American folk traditions, offering four public concerts that feature amazing musicians who have performed at Lincoln Center with the NY Philharmonic.
